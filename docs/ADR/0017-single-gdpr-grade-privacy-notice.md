# ADR 0017: One GDPR-grade privacy notice

Status: Accepted (CONFIRMED 2026-10-16, M6). The lawful bases inside the notice
are PROPOSED. Source: M6 README, M6-09.

## Context
Testers may be in several jurisdictions. Serbia's ZZPL is modeled on the GDPR.

## Decision
One privacy notice at GDPR level covers all testers, including those under
Serbia's ZZPL. Lawyer review is recommended before M7 and is not an M6 gate.

## Alternatives
None recorded.

## Consequences
The notice is marked "Draft — not reviewed by a lawyer." The controller name,
contact address, SMTP provider and host are placeholders or "to be named".

## How it is tested
Not by code. Links and placeholders are checked at merge.

## Rollback
Not recorded.
